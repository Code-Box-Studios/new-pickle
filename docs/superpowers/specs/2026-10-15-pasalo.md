# Pasalo: player booking transfers

## Approved scope

Players can offer a confirmed, paid, future booking to another player at a lower price. The entire booking transfers; the venue does not approve the transfer and retains its original payment. The existing reservation continues to occupy the court throughout the process. Only local Pikol bookings qualify; Sentry reservations require a separate bridge capability.

## First-release payment and communication

The first release uses direct GCash or Maya payment to the seller. A buyer reserves a listing for ten minutes and opens a private conversation with its seller. Payment account details appear only to these participants. The buyer marks payment sent; the seller checks their own wallet and explicitly confirms receipt before Pikol transfers ownership. Neither a screenshot nor a buyer's declaration verifies payment. Pikol does not collect fees, hold money, automatically verify payments, or promise refunds. PayMongo seller disbursements remain a separate integration.

A payment declaration freezes the listing until the seller completes the transfer or the booking becomes invalid. It cannot silently reopen while money may be in transit. Participants retain their conversation to resolve direct-payment issues. Text conversations are private to the buyer and seller; venue and administrative roles do not confer chat access.

## Integrity and access

Listings require a published approved venue, active court, confirmed online local PHP booking, and an original payment covering its price. Offers close thirty minutes before the actual Manila start time. Prices must be positive whole centavos below the original paid booking price. One listing and one completed resale are allowed per booking in this release.

All claim, transfer, cancellation, and rescheduling operations serialize against the same booking lock. Only one claim may be active. Transfer replaces the booking holder and contact information, generates a fresh reference, and preserves the venue payment and original reservation. The seller loses booking-holder access. The buyer sees their Pasalo receipt, never the original payer's payment reference or proof.

An unsold listing must be withdrawn before the holder cancels or reschedules. Normal venue cancellation or rescheduling remains available and invalidates the listing and outstanding claim without an approval step. Completed transfers are idempotent. Listings and claims expire using actual UTC instants, accounting for the application's Manila wall-clock storage convention.

## Interface

Provide public discovery, an offer detail page, optional buyer contact entry at claim time, a private trade conversation, and a personal selling/buying view. Eligible booking pages offer a compact listing form. Show the fixed venue, court, date, duration, original price, discount, and transfer price clearly. Use the existing shadcn components, teal and green design, mobile layouts, and reduced-motion support.

## Acceptance

Tests must cover ownership, unpaid or invalid bookings, Manila time boundaries, double claims, stale claims, payment declaration versus actual transfer, seller-only receipt confirmation, changed reservations, idempotency, private conversations, original receipt redaction, and stale seller mutation races. Player rosters and statistics are scoped separately in `2026-10-15-player-statistics.md` and are not implemented by this feature.
